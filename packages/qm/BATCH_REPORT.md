### Design decisions (please confirm)

1. **Rejected lot = usage decision valuation 'R'** (QM-002). Lots without a usage decision are counted separately and excluded from rates.
2. **Characteristic results depend on the lot module** (hard dependency) to get material, plant and supplier through the association.
3. **Quality notifications are modeled like PM notifications** (processing phase 1-3 = open), but as their own module and view.
4. **Supplier quality uses goods-receipt lots only** (origin '01', QM-R01).
