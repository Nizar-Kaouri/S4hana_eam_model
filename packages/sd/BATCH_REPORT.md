### Design decisions (please confirm)

1. **Sales orders only** (category C); quotations, contracts and returns are excluded for now.
2. **On time = goods issue posted on or before the planned goods issue date** (header level).
3. **Revenue = billing item net value**, negative for credit memos ('O') and cancellations ('N'), cancelled documents excluded (SD-021, SD-R01).
4. **Open sales order value = full item value** of open, non-rejected items; partial deliveries are not deducted yet.

### Open risks

| Path expressions in WHERE / case in revenue cube (SD-R01) | Revenue cube fails activation | Fallback: join the header explicitly |
| Billing volume (`sizeCategory: #XL`) | Slow queries | Filter on sales organization and billing date |
