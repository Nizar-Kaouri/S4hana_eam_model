### Design decisions (please confirm)

1. **All ledgers are kept** in the G/L cube; the query filters by ledger.
2. **Open item = no clearing document** (today's view). Open items at a past key date need clearing date logic and are a refinement.
3. **Aging buckets are fixed** (not due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, >90 days after net due date).
4. **Supplier and customer are separate modules** (FI view of the business partner); MM and SD use them through the links later.
