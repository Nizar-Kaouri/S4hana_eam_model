### Design decisions (please confirm)

1. **Material = `I_Product`.** S/4HANA uses "product" for the material master; element names follow `I_Product` (Product, ProductType, ProductGroup).
2. **Stock from `I_MaterialStock_2`** (quantities only). Stock value needs material valuation (price per plant), which is left for a refinement.
3. **Goods movements are split by debit/credit indicator** into receipts and issues (quantity and value), not by movement type. Movement type stays a characteristic.
4. **Open purchase order = item not completely delivered and not deleted.** The open value is the full item value. Partial deliveries are not deducted yet.
5. **Material consumption** uses the standard consumption movement types 201/221/261/281 and their reversals. Customer movement types must be added.
