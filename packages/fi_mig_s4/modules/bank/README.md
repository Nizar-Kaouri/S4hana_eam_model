# Bank master


Bank country and key identify the bank; preserve country-specific key formats and deletion status. House banks, house-bank accounts, bank account management and customer/supplier bank details are distinct objects and require their own approved interface/configuration. Do not treat bank master as a bank account balance.

## Deutsche Anleitung

Bankland und Bankschlüssel identifizieren die Bank. Länderspezifische Formate und Löschstatus erhalten. Hausbanken, Hausbankkonten, Bank Account Management und Partnerbankdaten sind eigenständige Objekte. Bankstamm nicht mit Bankkontostand verwechseln.

See customer/coverage-requirements.json, source mapping and the native DDIC preflight. No SAP activation or posting test has run.
