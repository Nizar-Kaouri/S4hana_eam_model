### Design decisions (please confirm)

1. **Master data valid today.** Cost centers and profit centers keep only the interval valid on the system date; history is a refinement.
2. **Primary vs. secondary costs by G/L account type** ('P' / 'S') via `I_GLAccountInChartOfAccounts` (CO-030).
3. **Actuals only.** Plan (ACDOCP) and commitments are a later refinement.
4. **Year-to-date report takes the prior year as its own parameter** to avoid arithmetic on NUMC parameters.
